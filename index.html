Privacy Policy — MotoDoc

Last updated: 1 September 2026

MotoDoc (“the app”) is a motorcycle maintenance tracker. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. Package name: com.alexde.motodoc.

Short version

Everything you enter — motorcycles, service records, insurance policies, refuelling entries and attached policy files — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics or advertising SDK in the app. We cannot see your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Motorcycle details: make, model, year, mileage and optional VIN.
  • Service records: type, date, mileage, workshop, cost, notes.
  • Insurance policies: type, company, policy number, dates, cost and any photo or PDF you attach.
  • Refuelling entries: litres, price per litre, mileage, date.
  • App settings and your subscription status.

This data lives in a local database inside the app’s private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app. The app does not create backups on our side; any backup is the one your phone or Google account makes.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to warn you when a service item becomes overdue or an insurance policy expires. Notifications are generated on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Photos and files — only when you choose to attach a photo or PDF of an insurance policy. The app copies the file you pick into its own storage and reads nothing else.
  • Internet — used for two things only: the Google Play billing check for your subscription, and loading the small list of partner links shown in the app.

Subscriptions and payments

MotoDoc is sold as an auto-renewing subscription with a 3-day free trial, billed through Google Play. Payments, card details and billing history are handled entirely by Google — the app never sees or stores payment data. It only asks Google Play whether your subscription is currently active and caches that answer locally so the app works offline. Manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

Third parties

  • Google Play Billing — processes the subscription. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Partner links — the app downloads a short list of links (workshops, insurers, fuel services) to display as buttons. Opening one hands you over to your browser and to that website’s own policy. No information about you is sent when the list is downloaded.

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, because we do not have it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete individual records inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Since nothing is kept on our side, there is nothing further to request.
