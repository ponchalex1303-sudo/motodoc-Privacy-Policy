Last updated: 24 September 2026

Short version

Everything you enter — motorcycles and their photos, service records, insurance policies and attachments, refuelling entries — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see your data.

What is stored on your device

  • Motorcycle details: make, model, year, mileage, optional VIN and a photo you choose.
  • Service records: type, date, mileage, workshop, cost, notes.
  • Insurance policies: type, company, number, dates, cost and any photo or PDF you attach.
  • Refuelling entries: litres, price per litre, mileage, date.

This data lives in a local database inside the app’s private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to warn you when a service item is overdue or a policy expires. They are generated on your device.
  • Photos and camera — only when you choose to add a photo of your motorcycle or an insurance policy. The file is copied into the app’s own storage.

MotoDoc is an auto-renewing subscription with a 3-day free trial, billed by the App Store on iOS and by Google Play on Android. Payments, card details and billing history are handled entirely by Apple or Google — the app never sees or stores payment data. It only asks the store whether your subscription is active and caches that answer locally so the app works offline. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Third parties

Only the store that processes your subscription: Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, because we do not have it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete individual records inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Nothing is kept on our side, so there is nothing further to request.
